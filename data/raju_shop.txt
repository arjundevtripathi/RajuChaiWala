--------------------------------------------------------------
ABOUT US
--------------------------------------------------------------

Raju Tea Shop is a beloved family-owned tea stall located near
Varanasi Railway Station, just opposite Platform 1's main exit
gate. Founded in 1998 by Mr. Raju Prasad, the shop has been
serving authentic Banarasi chai for over 25 years.

We are known for our:
  • Traditional masala chai made with fresh ginger and cardamom
  • Crispy samosas fried fresh every 2 hours
  • Warm, welcoming atmosphere for locals and travelers
  • Affordable prices that haven't changed much in a decade

Our shop is a favourite stopping point for:
  • Railway passengers waiting for their trains
  • College students from BHU and nearby institutes
  • Local rickshaw drivers and shopkeepers
  • Early morning walkers along the Ganga ghats

OUR STORY
--------------------------------------------------------------

Raju Prasad started this tea shop in 1998 with just a small
wooden cart and a dream. Over 25 years, it has grown into a
beloved local institution. Many of our customers today were
children when they first visited with their parents — and now
they bring their own children.

Raju's philosophy is simple:
  "Chai is not just a drink. It is a moment of peace in a
  busy day. Everyone who comes here should leave with a
  smile and a warm stomach."

Our chai recipe has remained unchanged for 25 years — the
same blend of Assam tea, fresh ginger, green cardamom, and
a secret family masala mix.
